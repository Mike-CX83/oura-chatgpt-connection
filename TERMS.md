# Terms of use

Updated November 6, 2026.

## Private integration

This integration is operated for its owner's personal use with their own Oura and ChatGPT accounts. It is not offered as a hosted service to the public. It connects a separately operated local Oura MCP service to ChatGPT through an OpenAI Secure MCP Tunnel, independently of any dashboard application.

## Authorization

Only data the account owner authorizes through Oura may be accessed. The owner controls the local service, credentials, tunnel, ChatGPT connection and revocation. Registration alone does not establish a working connection or authorize retrieval of records. Do not submit another person's records or credentials to this integration.

Oura's applicable API agreement and account terms govern Oura access. OpenAI's applicable account terms govern ChatGPT and its tunnel services. The selected open-source server is https://github.com/proscar87/oura-mcp and its software is subject to its own license. This documentation does not change those agreements or imply endorsement by Oura or OpenAI.

## Availability and interpretation

This personal integration has no service-level commitment. Access depends on the local computer, network, MCP process, tunnel, credentials and upstream services. Data may be incomplete, delayed or unavailable. Synthetic test data must be identified as synthetic. Tool results and AI interpretations are informational and are not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional evaluation.

## Privacy and discontinuation

The accompanying PRIVACY.md explains the intended data flow and controls. The owner may discontinue the integration by stopping its runtime, disconnecting it in ChatGPT and revoking Oura authorization. Existing records held by third-party services are managed through those services' controls.
